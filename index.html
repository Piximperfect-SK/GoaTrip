LEG 01 — RAIL

Pune Jn → Londa Jn

Overnight train, roughly 8–9 hours through the Western Ghats. Board late evening in Pune, wake up to fog over the hills as you roll into Londa around sunrise. Keep boarding‑pass details handy — seat numbers go on your digital pass below.

DEPARTS

Pune, ~21:30

ARRIVES

Londa, ~06:00

LEG 02 — ROAD

Londa Jn → Villa

Shared cabs pick up right outside Londa station for the ~3 hour drive down through the Ghats into Goa — cashew groves, hairpin bends, and your first smell of the coast. Group cabs will be arranged and shared here once headcount is final.

DRIVE TIME

~3 hrs

DROP

Villa gate

LEG 03 — RETURN

Villa → Londa Jn → Pune

The same route in reverse: cabs from the villa back up to Londa Jn, then the overnight train home to Pune. Exact pickup time depends on the return train — will be confirmed and added to everyone's boarding pass.
